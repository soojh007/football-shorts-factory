## Interpretation

The new model got 5 of 10 outcomes and 3 exact scores right, against 4 outcomes
and 2 exact scores for the previous recent-form model. Its mean point-score error
was 0.85 goals per team versus 1.10 for the previous model. It also improved
outcome Brier score and log loss against that model.

The league-averages-only baseline got 6 outcomes and 3 exact scores right. The
new model had slightly better outcome probability metrics, but slightly worse
scoreline log loss than that simpler baseline. This is a mixed result, not proof
of superiority. There were six home wins, two draws and two away wins in the
round; a blanket home-win selection therefore achieved 60% outcome accuracy.

Nottingham Forest's 3–1 win over Manchester City (model 0–1) and Manchester
United's 4–4 draw with Aston Villa (model 2–1) were conspicuous misses. No model
coefficients were changed in response to them. A larger, chronological multi-round
comparison is needed before tuning or making calibration claims.

league model. Team home/away sample sizes and fixture provenance are saved in
the local results JSON. This tests the **core league model**, not the complete
production prediction with historical player-rating/lineup overlays.

## Reproduce

```bash
python -m backend.backtest --league 8 --round 10 --season-id 28083 \
  --date-from 2026-11-01 --date-to 2026-11-15 \
  --output generated/backtests/epl_2026_27_round10
```

Fetching uses `SPORTMONKS_API_TOKEN` from the ignored `.env`. The output folder
contains the raw `input.json`, `results.json`, `matches.csv` and `report.md`.
To replay exactly without network or credentials:

```bash
python -m backend.backtest \
  --input generated/backtests/epl_2026_27_round10/input.json \
  --output generated/backtests/epl_2026_27_round10_replay
```

No OpenAI calls, videos or uploads are involved. Raw provider snapshots remain
local and ignored by Git. This committed report contains results and aggregates,
not API credentials or private video data.
